🚨 Financial Emergency Mode ⓘ
Emergency Mode Guide

🚨 What is Emergency Mode?
Emergency Mode is designed for situations where your available money is under significant pressure.

When can it activate?
Moneviax may recommend Emergency Mode when projected cash flow indicates that upcoming bills or spending could put your Safe to Spend below a critical level.

What does it do?
It creates a temporary Recovery Plan to help stabilize your finances.

Example:
You have $300 Safe to Spend, but upcoming obligations could leave you −$150.

🚨 Emergency Mode Recommended

Important: Emergency Mode does not automatically move money, cancel payments, contact creditors, or make financial decisions for you.

A simplified protection view for periods when projected cash flow becomes seriously constrained.
